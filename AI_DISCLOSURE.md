# AI DISCLOSURE

This project is designed and maintained by its owner, who sets its direction, makes the design and implementation decisions, and decides what tradeoffs are acceptable. Claude is used to write much of the code and draft changes. Every change is reviewed by the maintainer and playtested in-game before release.

The prebuilt `patch_northgard.exe` is built from the source in `tools/patch_northgard/`, only modifies Northgard's `hlboot.dat`, and can be fully reverted to vanilla with its restore command.
